How it works

Two signatures.
One transaction.

No escrow, no custody, no program holding your bag. A Stokz offer is a Solana transaction both sides sign. Until the second signature lands, nothing moves.

01

Pick any bag

Open a StonkFun stock and see its biggest holders, live from the chain. Pick a wallet, or paste one.

02

Sign your half

Set the amount and your price in SOL. You sign the trade and get a link. Nothing leaves your wallet yet.

03

They sign, it settles

Send the link. When they sign, the stock and the SOL swap in one transaction. The pool never sees it, so the price doesn't move.

Nobody holds anything

Your offer is pre-signed against a durable nonce, a one-time ticket in your own wallet (about 0.0015 SOL rent, refundable). Cancel any time: burn the ticket and the offer is dead.

If they never sign, nothing happens. If they sign, both sides settle in the same block, or neither does.

FAQ

Questions

Does Stokz ever hold my tokens or SOL?

No. There is no escrow and no Stokz program. An offer is a normal Solana transaction that moves the stock from the seller to the buyer and the SOL from the buyer to the seller. It only settles once both of you have signed it.

Why doesn't the price move?

The trade goes wallet to wallet and never touches the StonkFun pool, so the chart has nothing to react to. The pool price stays exactly where it was.

What if they never accept?

Nothing happens. Your SOL or tokens stay in your wallet. Cancel the offer in My offers to burn its nonce and get the ~0.0015 SOL rent back.

What does it cost?

The Solana network fee, plus about 0.0015 SOL of nonce rent per offer, which you get back when you cancel or reclaim. The buyer pays ~0.002 SOL to open a token account if they don't have one. Stokz takes no fee. StonkFun reward coins keep their own transfer fee, which is taken from the tokens moved.

Can I make an offer on any wallet?

Yes, on any wallet that holds the stock. They decide whether to accept. Pools and program accounts can't sign, so they can't take offers.

How do they find my offer?

You get a link that holds the signed offer. Send it to them or post it on X and tag them. Anyone with the link can check the terms and simulate the trade, but only the named wallet can complete it.

Can I get a partial fill?

No. An offer is for one exact amount at one exact price. To split a bag, make several offers.

Is Stokz part of StonkFun?

No. Stokz is an independent desk built on StonkFun's public data. StonkFun stocks are ordinary Solana tokens, so any two wallets can trade them directly.
